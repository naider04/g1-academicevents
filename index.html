About the Convention

INNOVA-ELT 2026 is an international academic convention focused on new pedagogical strategies for teaching English as a foreign language. It brings together English teachers, university students, researchers, and education professionals who want to improve their teaching practices and share innovative ideas.

Over three days in Quito, Ecuador, participants will explore modern teaching methods, discover new educational technologies, and promote collaboration among English language educators. Through conferences, practical workshops, and cultural activities, attendees will learn new strategies they can apply in their classrooms.

Themes and Research Areas

Gamification in ELT

Using games and playful strategies to enhance motivation and language acquisition.

Project-Based Learning

Integrating meaningful projects to develop communication and collaboration skills.

Communicative Language Teaching

Promoting real-life communication and authentic language use in the classroom.

Inclusive & Differentiated Teaching

Addressing diverse learner needs through inclusive and differentiated approaches.

ICT & AI in ELT

Leveraging educational technology and artificial intelligence for interactive learning.

Assessment for Learning

Practical tools for formative assessment, rubrics, checklists and self-assessment.

Call for Papers

We invite English teachers, university students, researchers, and education professionals to present their original work related to pedagogical innovation in English Language Teaching.

Participation Modes

  • Oral Presentation: 10-15 minute presentation followed by Q&A
  • Poster Presentation: Visual display of research/classroom innovation
  • Listener: Attendance without presentation
